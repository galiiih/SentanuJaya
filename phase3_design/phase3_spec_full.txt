==================================================

4. GOODS RECEIPT WORKFLOW
==================================================

Design workflow:

Customer membawa barang
↓
Admin membuat Surat Terima Barang / Goods Receipt
↓
Customer
↓
PO customer dicatat sebagai reference
↓
Item customer dicatat
↓
Quantity diterima
↓
Condition diperiksa
↓
Photo/document attachment bila diperlukan
↓
Barang masuk custody
↓
Custody ledger bertambah

Condition minimal harus mempertimbangkan:

- GOOD
- DENT
- CRACK
- OLD_PAINT
- OTHER

Jangan menambah status baru tanpa alasan.

Audit apakah schema saat ini mendukung condition tersebut.

==================================================
7. SHORTAGE / OVERPRODUCTION RULE
==================================================

Gunakan business rule yang sudah disepakati:

Jika customer merasa quantity kurang:

1. Check customer custody balance.
2. Check production/delivery status.
3. Check apakah ada overproduction item/color.
4. Contoh:
   Customer order black = 3
   Production menghasilkan black = 4

Extra 1 black dapat menjelaskan apparent shortage.

Jika benar-benar tidak ditemukan:

→ shortage/loss harus dapat dicatat.

Jika benar-benar hilang:

→ kompensasi berdasarkan item price sesuai business rule.

Jangan membuat compensation workflow pada Phase 3 jika belum diperlukan.

Tetapi schema harus memungkinkan shortage/loss dicatat di masa depan.
